Awesome Agentic On-Policy Distillation

This collection requires both an interactive agent task and an on-policy distillation objective. The agent executes actions through tools, software, physical/simulated environments, or task-level communication with users and other agents, and uses the returned feedback. Static multimodal QA and screenshot grounding alone are outside scope. Offline training contexts are allowed when the resulting policy is evaluated in closed-loop interaction.
